Client Management

Learn how to play the game with the client utility!

📄️Client Introduction

The client utility is used to play the actual game. The server alone cannot be used to play Space Merchant as it is only a console app.

📄️Choosing Entity To Play

When you sucessfully connect to a server, it will present you with a list of playable entities. You will then have to choose one entity to play.

📄️Gamespace Navigation

Gamespace is the term used to describe the simulations enviroment. For more information about the gamespace, visit its section in the server chapter.

📄️Market

There are 2 possible operations to do on a market: buy listed trade offers or post your own offer.

📄️Logistics

In the logistics section, you can view your local containers and view their contents.
